# Evidence levels

The project uses explicit evidence labels to prevent representational patterns from being confused with cryptographic leakage.

## DEMONSTRATED_MATHEMATICALLY
A result follows from a mathematical derivation under stated assumptions.

## FORMALLY_VERIFIED
A result has been checked in a proof assistant or equivalent formal system.

## REPRODUCED_COMPUTATIONALLY
A result has been independently reproduced by code on defined inputs.

## STATISTICAL_EVIDENCE
A measured effect exceeds an explicit statistical baseline, with stated controls and uncertainty.

## HEURISTIC
A potentially useful rule or pattern without proof or sufficient statistical validation.

## HYPOTHESIS
A proposed explanation or research direction that remains unverified.

## REFUTED
A proposed explanation has a better demonstrated cause, counterexample, or failed out-of-sample validation.

## UNKNOWN
Available evidence is insufficient to classify the claim.

## Mandatory distinction

A bijective deterministic representation does not add entropy. In particular, converting one BIP-39 index into a local position, an odd-prime ordinal, or the corresponding odd prime does not by itself reduce the BIP-39 search space.
